Ritual Made Simple

How to Brew

Three steps. One mindful moment. Centuries of goodness in your hands.

🥄
1

Scoop

Measure one heaped teaspoon (approx. 5g) of Ramu Jamu powder into your favourite mug.

💧
2

Add Hot Water

Pour 200ml of freshly boiled water (90–95°C) over the powder. Let it bloom for 10 seconds.

✨
3

Stir & Enjoy

Stir gently until dissolved. Breathe in the aroma. Sip slowly, and let the herbs work their magic.

Our Story

Rooted in the Archipelago, Brewed for the Straits

Jamu has been Indonesia's gift to the world for over a thousand years — herbal wisdom passed from ibu to anak, from the royal courts of Yogyakarta to the hawker lanes of Singapore's Kampong Glam.

Ramu Jamu was born from a simple conviction: that this heritage deserved a place in the modern wellness ritual. We work with small-batch farmers in Java and Sumatra, sourcing turmeric, ginger, lemongrass, and pandan at their peak — then gently powdering them to preserve every volatile oil, every healing compound.

No compromise. No shortcuts. Just the earth, the roots, and time.
